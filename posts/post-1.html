My little corner of the internet!

Posted 6 November 2026

Welcome to my little corner of the web! I have always wanted to blog regularly and attempted it back in middle school, alas, only to lose the novelty of web design and prose writing, thereby shifting to something more glamorous like programming in VB (my first programming encounter) or building wired RC cars with DC motors (wireless was out of middle school me's league). But lately I've had this subtle feeling that my writing skills are deteriorating, given the era of ignored grammar and vocabulary in most digital conversations and cheap LLM-generated rambles.

These days, I mostly find myself doing math and programming. I'm quite a novice in both, yet I derive so much pleasure from learning and solving problems. That's all for the first post!
